How to Play 💞

⌨️ Keyboard

Move — A/D or ◀ ▶
Jump — W / ▲ / Space (press again to double-jump)
Attack — J or Z
Special — K or X (uses MP)
Weapon Skill — U, O, or B (needs a weapon)
Heart ❤ — L or C or E
Pick / Drop Weapon — Q

📱 Touch

Left side — joystick.
Right side — Jump, Attack, Special, Weapon Skill, Heart, and Pick/Drop buttons.

💞 The Heart Button

Stand close to each other, then…
Tap ❤ — hold hands: faster, protected, hearts everywhere.
Hold ❤ — hug: heals you both.
Full Love Meter + ❤ — KISS! A love burst clears the screen and fully heals you.
If one of you falls, the other can revive with a hug and kiss (hold ❤ next to them).

Fill the Love Meter by fighting together, staying close, and collecting hearts. Joku 🌊 attacks with water and dashes through danger. Jolie 🌸 fires petals and blooms a healing field. Weapons do not auto-pickup: stand near one and press Pick/Drop, then use Weapon Skill for its extra power. Open Settings to review all weapon skills and roles. Your supporters fight too — 🐶 Lulu bites nearby shadows, 🐼 Biscuit tosses healing hearts when you're hurt. Watch out for dive-bombing imps, and jump the boss's shockwaves!
