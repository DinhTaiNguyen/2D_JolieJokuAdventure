How to Play 💞

⌨️ Keyboard

Move — A/D or ◀ ▶
Jump — W / ▲ / Space (press again to double-jump)
Attack — J or Z
Special — K or X (uses MP)
Heart ❤ — L or C or E

📱 Touch

Left side — joystick.
Right side — Jump, ⚔ Attack, ✦ Special, 💗 Heart buttons.

💞 The Heart Button

Stand close to each other, then…
Tap ❤ — hold hands: faster, protected, hearts everywhere.
Hold ❤ — hug: heals you both.
Full Love Meter + ❤ — KISS! A love burst clears the screen and fully heals you.
If one of you falls, the other can revive with a hug (hold ❤ next to them).

Fill the Love Meter by fighting together, staying close, and collecting hearts. Joku 🌊 shoots water phoenixes and dashes on wings. Jolie 🌸 fires petals and blooms a healing field. Your supporters fight too — 🐶 Kai bites nearby shadows, 🐼 Momo tosses healing hearts when you're hurt. Watch out for dive-bombing imps, and jump the boss's shockwaves!
